Three Domain Names

A Website Where Vapers Quit for Life.


  • puffclear.live
  • puffclear.life
  • quitvape.life

My classmates liked my idea of using either the .live or .life extension because it seems health-conscious. We discussed other .com and .net extensions, but these domains are more expensive, and in some ways, less modern. We want to signal that we are on the cutting-edge to potential vape quitters, and so using an uncommon extension helps. Its also easy to remember .life or .live. My classmate was the one that came up with puffclear, and I like it, but I don't want to make it seem like vaping is a good thing.


I think I will end up choosing puffclear.life over quitvape.life, only because it sounds more modern and less judgemental. Quitvape is a very direct name, but not everyone is ready to fully make the plunge into quitting from the start. I want my app to help people become aware of their habits and gradually quit so that they can be free long-term. Quitvape makes it sound like you have to go cold-turkey immediately, which would turn a lot of people off. I chose the .life over the .live extension because it seems to be catchier, and I want it to be around for life.
