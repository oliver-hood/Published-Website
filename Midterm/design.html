Design Statement

The Official North Korean Travel Website Design Guide. I hope that you had as much fun reading through my website as I did making it. I've always read articles, heard stories, and watched news reels about North Korea, and I thought that it would be fun to make a satirical website for this other-worldly country.

Design Overview

To create the website, I built the site layout first, and then let the aesthetics come out naturally. The structure takes inspiration from the iAmsterdam site, which gave me a grid and simple navigation. For the header, I’ve noticed that a lot of websites have it stuck to to the top, but I’m not a fan. For my typography hierarchy, I used the Perfect Fourth type scale because it looked the best and kept sizing simple and dynamic without much thought. I wanted to use Futura (North Korea = dystopian future) but licensing (capitalism) blocked it, so I used Lato for headings because it is similar and downloadable on Google Fonts. For paragraphs I used Baskervville, which is like a more modern Times New Roman and keeps the paragraphs readable.

For visuals I tried to replicate a bit of Apple’s “liquid glass” aesthetic. I used rounded corners and a light box around text to lift the text off the page and improve contrast. I chose North Korean red and blue for the palette, an obvious choice, but needed the light boxes to keep it readable. I generated the code for the gradient using one of the tools found here . The images use consistent sizing and rounded corners, and I spaced them evenly from headings and paragraphs to keep alignment clean.

For the interactions I kept things simple and tactile. The “Request a Tour” button has a left to right underline animation and sits in a clear call to action spot (centered in the footer, because Americans can't go to North Korea). All of the links have a straightforward hover state, and buttons and nav items are sized to be easily clickable. I will admit I feel like an “internet thief” because I borrowed structures and designs from other websites that looked good, like the book a tour button and the layout, but I adapted them to fit this site’s tone and content.
