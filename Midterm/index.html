Discover The Deomocratic People's Republic

The Official North Korean Travel Guide. Step into the Democratic People's Republic of Korea and experience unmatched culture, exotic cuisine, and top-notch skiing. Because of the natural beauty, the DPRK is closed off to much of the world for preservation. Allow this website to show you the inner beauty prreviosuly unseen.

Disconnect and step into a country with no wireless connection. Put the phone down and recharge without distractions.

Marvel and literally eat off of the spotless streets. Pyongyang is the cleanest capital city anywhere in the world.

Experience beautiful light shows every night. The Korean People's Army routinely performs missile and artillery tests, better than the best firework shows.

Loose your depression in a "Spontaneous Hapiness Parade". Don't worry if you don't know the words, your tour guide will tell you when when its time to smile.
